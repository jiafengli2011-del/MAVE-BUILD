— Overview

One small building. Whatever you need it to be.

The Hearth Pod is the smallest thing we make and the most adaptable. A backyard office on Monday, a studio on Saturday, a room for the work that doesn't fit in the house — one clear space, fitted out for the life you actually have.

At 167 square feet, most jurisdictions require no permit at all. It arrives as a kit, stands in days, and its clean silhouette and warm plywood interior read as contemporary without shouting about it.
