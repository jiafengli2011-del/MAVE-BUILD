— Overview

Built for people who go outside.

The Grove Studio is designed around what surrounds it — a base camp that keeps you close to the land without asking you to rough it. A sloped roofline and a warm plywood interior set a tone that feels earned rather than decorated.

The loft sleeping area stays tucked above while the main level opens directly onto a deck, ready for the morning you're already thinking about before you fall asleep. Sleeps up to four. Packs in everything. Gets out of the way.
